bump: minor

- New value of the installation, `LIGHT_RUNNER` (`ubuntu-slim` by default): the runner label of the short jobs that need only bash, git, jq, the GitHub CLI and Node.js. GitHub bills every job rounded up to the minute, and on a private repository most of the bill of such jobs is rounding; the one-CPU runner costs less per minute. On it: `auto-merge`'s `promote-from-ci`, `ready-on-green`, the weekly digest, the `eco-design` audit (its timeout down from 30 to 15 minutes, that runner's limit) and the CI skeleton's `changes` job.
- `claude-review`'s `report` and `claude-rework`'s `prepare`, `propose` and `needs-human` run on `ubuntu-slim`, written in the files rather than a value: CR-1 and RW-1 pin those files by digest, and a digest that accepted any runner label would accept one for the job that runs Claude too. Both digests are updated for these four lines. The jobs that build, scan or run Claude stay on `ubuntu-latest`. `update <dir> --set LIGHT_RUNNER=ubuntu-latest` keeps the 2-core runner; a self-hosted label works too.
- `README.md` § Actions minutes: the levers (the light runner, skipped jobs, folded steps, public repositories and self-hosted runners, the billing usage API) and why the CI workflow does not cancel superseded runs where `auto-merge` is installed. `docs/module-authoring.md` § Workflows: a short job runs on `__HARNESS_LIGHT_RUNNER__`.

By hand: nothing. `update` renders the new value with its default; a project whose runners must stay on `ubuntu-latest`, or whose light jobs need more than the `ubuntu-slim` image carries, sets `--set LIGHT_RUNNER=ubuntu-latest`. A project that copied the `changes` job into its own CI workflow may give it `runs-on: ubuntu-slim` by hand. Where `claude-review` or `claude-rework` must keep `ubuntu-latest`, edit those `runs-on` lines back and set the digest CR-1 or RW-1 prints.
