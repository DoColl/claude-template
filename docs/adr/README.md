# Architecture decision records

One file per decision that will outlive its context: a technology, a pattern, a trade-off, a deliberate "we do not do this". An ADR records what was decided, what else was considered and what the decision costs — so that the next person (or session) does not reopen it without new facts.

- Copy `ADR-000-template.md` to `ADR-<nnn>-<slug>.md`; numbers are never reused.
- An ADR is not edited once accepted. A change of mind is a new ADR that supersedes it, or a dated addendum at the end.
- The operative part of a decision (the rule people follow day to day) also gets its bullet in `CLAUDE.md` and its file in `docs/conventions/`.
