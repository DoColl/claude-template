## Context

The situation that forces a choice: the constraint, the incident, the requirement. Facts, with their source.

## Decision

What is decided, stated so that someone can tell whether a given change respects it.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was seriously on the table and the reason it lost. "Rejected because" is the part people come back for.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must now be maintained, and what would make this decision worth revisiting.
